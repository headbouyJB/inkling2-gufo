# Baseline — 8bb6cd5 (0.1.2), image `localhost/gufo-runtime:inkling2`, 2026-11-22

Everything the perf work is measured against. Numbers come from
[`tests/perf/bench.py`](../../tests/perf/bench.py), run twice per case, on this
box (gfx1151, Ryzen AI MAX+ 395, 128 GiB unified). Raw samples:
[`baseline-standalone.json`](baseline-standalone.json),
[`baseline-swap.json`](baseline-swap.json).

## Results

| case | what it times | standalone :5830 | through llama-swap :8080 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `prefill-4k` | cold prefill of 4 097 tokens, 8 generated | **1 357** tok/s | **1 337** tok/s |
| `prefill-16k` | cold prefill of 16 330 tokens, 8 generated | **1 372** tok/s | **1 362** tok/s |
| `decode-1k` | decode with 1 056 tokens of context, 128 generated | **45.1** tok/s | **44.8** tok/s |
| `decode-16k` | decode with 16 329 tokens of context, 64 generated | **41.1** tok/s | **41.1** tok/s |
| `batch2-1k` | two concurrent 1 059-token requests, 128 generated each | **68.8** tok/s agg | **69.7** tok/s agg |

Median of 2 reps per cell; the largest spread over the two reps was 3.1 %
(`batch2-1k`, standalone), everything else ≤1.2 %. The two front doors agree
within 1.5 %, so later changes are compared on `:5830` and spot-checked on
`:8080`.

The same run reports the server's own `prompt_tokens_per_second` /
`completion_tokens_per_second` — the fields `test-serve.sh` and the llama-swap
logs print, which is why the numbers line up with the README's 46.6 tok/s.

## What the goal's targets mean from here

| target | baseline | needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 100 tok/s single-session decode | 44.8 (`decode-1k`), 41.1 (`decode-16k`) | **×2.23** at 1k, ×2.43 at 16k |
| 2 000 tok/s prefill | 1 337 (`prefill-4k`), 1 362 (`prefill-16k`) | **×1.47** |

The decode target cannot come from kernel efficiency alone: `lab/RESULTS.md`
measures the decode kernels at 207–239 GB/s against a 242 GB/s ceiling, so the
remaining factor has to come from the DSpark drafter (task-5) or from fewer
weight bytes read per accepted token.

## Method

* Prompt text is generated by `tests/perf/bench.py` from a fixed word list with
  `--seed 1234`; only the leading nonce changes per run, which is what keeps
  the prefix cache cold. A prefill case fails if the response reports
  `cached_tokens != 0`.
* `prefill-*` numbers are the server's `prompt_tokens_per_second` for a request
  whose prefix is cold; the generated 8 tokens are not counted.
* `decode-*` numbers are `completion_tokens_per_second`, i.e. decode only (the
  prefill is excluded by the server, the wall time is printed beside it).
* `batch2-1k` gates on the sum of the two requests' `decode_tokens_per_second`
  (the server reports each request's own window), with the wall-clock aggregate
  stored beside it as `wall_decode_tps`. 68.8–69.7 tok/s aggregate = 1.53–1.56×
  one session, i.e. batched decode already shares one weight pass.
* First request after a load is a warm-up and is discarded.

Reproduce:

```sh
# standalone (unload llama-swap first: it is swap-mode single resident)
./serve-standalone.sh
tests/perf/bench.py --reps 2 --out notes/perf/baseline-standalone.json
podman rm -f inkling2-serve

# through llama-swap (loads the model on the first request, ~74 s)
tests/perf/bench.py --base-url http://127.0.0.1:8080 --reps 2 \
  --out notes/perf/baseline-swap.json
```

## Known gaps in this baseline

* No in-process `inkling2_bench` number yet (task-2 builds it with the dev
  build); the e2e numbers above are the gate for now.
* Decode tok/s at 16k is 9 % below 1k — attention/state work with context
  length, which the per-stage map (task-2) is expected to quantify.
* Only measured at `--context 262144 --sessions 2`, the llama-swap flag set.
